 FAQs
 When should I apply?
 There&#x27;s no such thing as too early for Z Fellows. We&#x27;ve had many successful Z Fellows do the program while working on a number of side projects... 
 Should each team member fill out an application?
 You can fill out just one application for your team. If you have a co-founder, make sure to note this somewhere in your application. 
 More questions?
 Send them to [email&#160;protected] 
 Where do I have to be based?
 You can be based anywhere, there are Z Fellows all over the world. 
 Cohorts are mostly virtual, with the last day of the program in-person in SF or NYC. Most Z Fellows end up coming to SF or NYC for the entire week. 
 Do I need to have a company?
 No. We can help you set up one.
 Do you take equity in my company?
 It&#x27;s up to you. You can participate without money or we can give you a $10,000 investment at a $1B valuation cap.
 You can tell your friends your company is worth $1B ;).
 This $10,000 investment will convert into stock at your next priced round.
 Do I need a team to apply?
 No. You can apply solo or with your co-founders.
 Do I need to drop out or quit my job?
 No.
 What’s the timeline?
 Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and there is no deadline to submit an application.  We run multiple cohorts every year. Apply and you&#x27;ll be considered for the next one.
 If you&#x27;ve been rejected before, you can re-apply now. We encourage it :).
 Who can apply?
 There have been Z Fellow high school dropouts, college students, and people with full-time jobs .... there&#x27;s no age restriction to apply!
 More questions?
 Text Cory (the founder) at 650-505-9984 
 When in doubt, apply.
We’re all winging it :)

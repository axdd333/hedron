 Innovation Spotlight
 Innovation Spotlight provides startups and early-stage businesses an opportunity to network with key resources in Connecticut, present their business to a room of potential investors and growth partners, and receive grant funding for innovation-driven projects.
 The goal is to spotlight innovative technologies, foster relationships local to Connecticut’s advanced industry sectors, and give entrepreneurs project-specific funding to dream big and push the limits of creation.
 Applicants will be reviewed in stages, and although not all applicants will be invited to pitch their company and project at the Innovation Spotlight event, we encourage all applicants to attend these in person functions.

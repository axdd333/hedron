# NSF I-Corps Hub Northeast Region

![Image 3: Person standing and presenting to a group of I-Corps participants](https://icorpsnortheasthub.org/sites/g/files/toruqf8456/files/styles/8x3_1440w_540h/public/2026-08/Accelerate_UConn_Kickoff%20-82.jpg?itok=ilxMsLka)

## Great Innovations Start with Discovery

Through customer discovery, entrepreneurial training, and a network of experienced mentors, the NSF I-Corps Northeast Hub helps innovators transform promising research into solutions people need.

## Meet the Northeast Hub

The NSF I-Corps Hub: Northeast Region is made up of 10 universities, with new partner institutions added annually.

Princeton University is the principal institution in the Hub, along with 9 partner institutions including: the University of Delaware, Rutgers University, Lehigh University, Temple University, New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), Rowan University, Drexel University, Yale University and the University of Connecticut. The Hub will expand by adding new partner institutions each year.

Located in the heart of the U.S. Northeast, the Hub makes use of its proximity to “deep-tech industries” that revolve around fundamental discoveries in areas such as health care and pharmaceuticals, energy, the environment, earth- and water-friendly “green and blue” technologies, financial technologies, agriculture, and communications and digital information. _**Note: Participants in Hub programs do NOT need to be affiliated with a Hub member university.**_

## The NSF I-Corps Journey at the Northeast Hub

| Path | Program | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Step 1: Learn | [Novus](https://icorpsnortheasthub.org/programs/novus) | Half day workshop designed to introduce individuals and teams to the concept of customer discovery. |
| Step 2: Test | [Propelus](https://icorpsnortheasthub.org/programs/propelus) [Themed Propelus cohorts:](https://icorpsnortheasthub.org/events/2026/propelus-i-corps-hosted-princeton-university-sbir-theme) [SBIR Propelus](https://icorpsnortheasthub.org/events/2026/propelus-i-corps-hosted-princeton-university-sbir-theme) | 4-week introductory program designed to support teams as they are uncovering their customer segments and value propositions. |
| Step 3: Discover | [National I-Corps Program](https://icorpsnortheasthub.org/programs/national-i-corps-teams-program) (Deep tech teams with university IP) [Ascendus](https://icorpsnortheasthub.org/programs/ascendus)(All other teams) | 7-week program designed to continue the deep dive into customer discovery through intensive training and more than 100 customer interviews. |
| Step 4: Launch | [Lean Startup Accelerator](https://icorpsnortheasthub.org/programs/lean-startup-accelerator-lsa) | 9-week program dedicated to launching promising startups from the Northeast Hub. |

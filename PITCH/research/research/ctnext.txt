 Connecticut&#8217;s Office of Innovation
 The Office of Innovation is dedicated to the activation of Connecticut’s full economic potential by providing the local innovation ecosystem with support at all levels, from ideation through commercialization.
 We deliver programming and initiatives that ensure Connecticut’s industries remain competitive, generate new high-paying jobs, revitalize our cities as centers of innovation, promote equitable growth for residents and communities, and keep us at the cutting edge of technology in a rapidly evolving world.
